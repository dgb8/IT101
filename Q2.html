Pacific Northwest X-Ray Inc

Here are 10 reasons that http://www2.pnwx.com/ is a badly designed website.

1: Almost the entire webpage is just a background image.

2: Its search bar requires you to already know the name of the product you are looking for rather than giving you a way to look it up.

3: The text color blends into the background color.

4: The yellow underlines beneath the listing in the popular section cut partway through the bottom of the text.

5: The company name and quote at the top of the page are very small for something that should be attention grabbing.

6: The quote is in a font style and size that makes it hard to read.

7: The company name and quote are in different colors that don’t compliment each other or the background.

8: None of the images next to the popular listings are different to help distinguish them or show what the products looks like.

9: The webpage doesn’t actually give you that much information about the products or company.

10: The color scheme of the webpage in general is to bright and garish.
